ClipBot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

What ClipBot is

ClipBot is a private tool operated by WastedClipsTV. It prepares short video clips and uploads them to the WastedClipsTV YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

ClipBot uses YouTube API Services. By using ClipBot you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we access

ClipBot only requests permission to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel (scope youtube.upload). It does not read, collect or store personal information, viewing history, comments or other channel data.

Data storage and sharing

The authorization token is stored only on the computer where ClipBot runs. ClipBot does not send it to any server other than Google's, and does not share, sell or transfer any data to third parties.

Revoking access

You can revoke ClipBot's access at any time from your Google Account security settings: myaccount.google.com/connections. After revocation the stored token stops working; it can also be deleted from the computer at any time.
